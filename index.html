About HarvestLink

FROM THE FARM TO THE RIGHT HANDS.

HarvestLink is a digital platform designed to make agricultural produce easier to discover, sell and distribute.

Farmers can create listings for their produce while buyers can browse available products, compare prices and place orders through the platform.

View Marketplace

How It Works

ONE PLATFORM. THREE SIMPLE STEPS.

01

Farmers List

Farmers add their produce, quantity, price, location and other details.

02

Buyers Discover

Buyers search and filter available agricultural products on the marketplace.

03

Orders Connect

Buyers place orders and farmers can manage them through their dashboard.
